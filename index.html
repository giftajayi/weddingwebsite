Our Story

❤️ Her Story

Our story began in 2013 during our university days at Obafemi Awolowo University. It was a regular evening on campus, but one unexpected moment changed the course of our lives. After a long night of studying during a power outage, I was ready to return to my hostel. However, I hesitated because I did not want to walk back alone, so I decided to wait until someone else was leaving the classroom. That was when Funto was leaving. I introduced myself and asked if we could walk together. What started as a simple walk back to the hostel became a beautiful conversation that lasted over an hour. We talked about our studies, dreams, interests, and the things that mattered to us. At the time, I did not know that this small moment would become the beginning of something so special. What started as a friendship grew over time into a relationship built on love, understanding, and shared memories. Looking back, I am grateful for that unexpected evening and the journey it started. Today, I celebrate not only the love we share but also the friendship and connection that brought us here.

🤍 His Story

In 2013, while studying at Obafemi Awolowo University, I never imagined that an ordinary evening on campus would introduce me to someone who would become such an important part of my life. After a long day of studying, I was leaving the classroom when I met Gbemi. She approached me and asked if we could walk together back to the hostel. What seemed like a simple request turned into a conversation filled with laughter, curiosity, and getting to know each other. We talked about our dreams, our experiences, our interests, and the future we hoped to build. From that first conversation, I discovered someone who was thoughtful, genuine, and easy to connect with. Over the years, our friendship grew stronger. Through different seasons of life, distance, and challenges, we continued to choose each other and build something meaningful. What began as a simple walk became the foundation of a beautiful love story. I am grateful for the journey that brought us together and excited for the lifetime of memories we will continue to create as Gbemi and Funto.
